Our story

From a listings page to a trust platform

The observation
Across Madhya Pradesh, good farmland sits fallow beside farmers who want more land — because leasing it out feels like risking the land itself.
The start
farmkaro.in began as a simple listings site. It taught us the real problem was never discovery — it was trust: verified ownership, walked boundaries, and a lease that creates no tenancy rights.
The rebuild
FarmKaro was rebuilt from the ground up as a verification-first platform: khasra-anchored parcels, a document-and-boundary ladder, FarmSelect AI marking, and registered fixed-term leases under MP's framework.
Now
The Jabalpur pilot: first verified listings, first registered lease concluded through the platform, first district at operational break-even.

Our vision

Every acre working. Every lease on paper. Every farmer bankable.

A rural land market where trust is infrastructure, not luck — starting in Jabalpur, then district by district, each one standing on its own economics before the next.

What we are working on

  • Onboarding the first verified landowners across Jabalpur's seven tehsils
  • Field-executive playbook: documents checked, boundary walked, parcel marked verified
  • Lease documentation vetted by local advocates under MP's land-leasing framework
  • Land-records cooperation with the revenue department (MoU drafted)
  • Credit-linkage groundwork so a registered lease opens bank and insurance doors
