Why lease through FarmKaro

The paperwork is the product.

An honest verification ladder

Every parcel shows the exact rung it has reached. “Uploaded” is not “verified”, and we never blur the two.

Real land, on real satellite

Parcels sit at their true coordinates on live imagery. A surveyed boundary is drawn only once one genuinely exists.

Leases with an end date

Executed under the state framework: a fixed term, possession reverting on expiry, and no tenancy or occupancy rights created.

Published, transparent pricing

A public fee list, and rent ranges drawn only from leases that actually completed. No invented valuations.

How it works

Discover → Verify → Lease → Farm

  1. 01

    Discover

    Search by village or radius. Compare rent, water, road access and soil on one map.

  2. 02

    Verify

    Inspect documents, boundary status and the owner's identity checks before you commit.

  3. 03

    Lease

    Agree terms, sign digitally, and register the lease under the state framework.

  4. 04

    Farm

    Rent schedule, receipts and renewal reminders run in one place for both sides.
